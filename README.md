# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

End-to-end data analytics project using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, DAX, and Power BI to analyze pizza sales performance, product performance, revenue trends, and category-level sales through interactive business intelligence dashboards.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes one year of pizza sales transaction data to uncover meaningful business insights related to sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, pizza categories, pizza sizes, and customer ordering patterns.

The workflow starts with the raw sales dataset and uses Power Query for data preparation, DAX for KPI calculations, and Power BI for developing interactive dashboards that transform transactional data into clear and actionable business insights.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The restaurant has a large amount of daily pizza transaction data, but raw transactional data can make it difficult to understand overall business performance and product-level results.

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions such as:

- How much revenue did the restaurant generate?
- How many orders and pizzas were sold?
- Which months generated the highest revenue?
- Which days had the highest order activity?
- Which pizza categories contribute the most revenue?
- Which pizza sizes perform best?
- Which pizzas are the top-performing products?
- Which pizzas are consistently underperforming?

To address these questions, an interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to transform raw sales data into an easy-to-understand business reporting solution.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall pizza sales performance.
- Calculate key business KPIs.
- Analyze monthly and daily sales trends.
- Evaluate performance by pizza category and size.
- Identify top and bottom-performing pizza products.
- Compare product performance by revenue and quantity sold.
- Develop a professional two-page Power BI dashboard for business reporting.

# 📊 Dashboard Preview

### Dashboard 1 — Sales Overview

![Sales Performance Dashboard](04_Image/dashboard-home.png)

### Dashboard Description

This dashboard provides an overview of pizza sales performance through key performance indicators, revenue trends, daily order analysis, and category and size-level sales analysis.


### Key Performance Indicators
| KPI                      |        Value |
| ------------------------ | -----------: |
| Total Revenue            | **$817.86K** |
| Total Orders             |   **21,350** |
| Total Pizzas Sold        |   **49,574** |
| Average Order Value      |   **$38.31** |
| Average Pizzas per Order |     **2.32** |


### Dashboard Features

- Executive KPI Cards
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Orders by Day of Week
- Revenue by Pizza Category
- Revenue by Pizza Size
- Pizza Sales by Category
- Sales Performance Overview

### Key Business Insights

- Generated $817.86K in total revenue during the analysis period.
- Processed 21,350 orders.
- Sold 49,574 pizzas.
- July recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- Friday recorded the highest daily order activity.
- The Classic pizza category was one of the strongest contributors to overall sales.
- Large-size pizzas generated the highest share of total revenue.

## Dashboard 2 — Product Performance

![Sales Performance Dashboard](04_Image/dashboard-home.png)

### Dashboard Description

This dashboard focuses on individual pizza performance and compares the strongest and weakest products based on revenue and quantity sold.

### Dashboard Features

Top 5 Pizzas:

- Top 5 by Revenue
- Top 5 by Quantity

Bottom 5 Pizzas:

- Bottom 5 by Revenue
- Bottom 5 by Quantity

Category Performance:

- Revenue
- Quantity Sold
- Orders

### Key Business Insights

- **Thai Chicken Pizza** and **Barbecue Chicken Pizza** were among the highest-revenue products.
- **Classic Deluxe Pizza** recorded the highest quantity sold among the products shown in the analysis.
- **Brie Care Pizza** appeared among the lowest-performing products across the analyzed performance metrics.
- The dashboard makes it easier to identify products that contribute strongly to sales as well as products that may require further business attention.
